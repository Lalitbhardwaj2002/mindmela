Kaise khelein

  1. Ek sawaal aayega, do jawaab ke saath. Apna jawaab chuno.
  2. Andaaza lagao kitne log tumhare jaisa chunenge (khud ko bhi gino).
  3. Ekdum sahi andaaza = 3 number. Ek se chooke = 1 number.
  4. Phir sabko dikhega kisne kya chuna. Ab ho jaaye behes!
