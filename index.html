What is Mindmela?

Mindmela (Mind Mela) is a free online desi party game for 2–12 players: a mela of minds, where everyone brings an opinion and the fun is guessing who thinks like you. Each round asks a funny Hinglish question — Sarkari naukri ya private? Meerut ki gajak ya Khurja ki khurchan? — and you guess how many of your friends picked the same answer as you.

How to play

  1. One person creates a room and shares the 4-letter code or QR code.
  2. Everyone joins from their own phone or laptop. No sign-up, no download.
  3. Pick your side, then guess how many people picked the same. Exactly right scores 3 points; off by one scores 1.
  4. See who picked what, argue about it, and win awards like The Rebel and Twin Souls.

Thousands of desi questions

Question packs include Desi Bulandshahr, Meerut Side, Family, Grown-ups and Hot Takes, with over 9,000 questions about food, weddings, relatives, jobs, villages and more. Play a quick game or go endless. Mindmela works in any browser and can be installed on your phone like an app.

How to play

  1. A desi question pops up with two answers. Pick yours.
  2. Guess how many people picked the same as you (count yourself).
  3. Exactly right scores 3 points. Off by one scores 1.
  4. Then everyone sees who picked what. Let the arguments begin.

Most points at the end wins, plus awards like The Rebel and Twin Souls. In an endless game, the host decides when to stop.
